What to Expect From a Free Roof Inspection

No mystery, no pressure - here's exactly how ours works.

Fluid Roofing · Spokane & Airway Heights, WA

"Free inspection" can sound like code for "hard sales pitch." It shouldn't be. Here's what a real, honest roof inspection looks like - and what you should walk away with.

What we check

  • The shingles or surface - wear, curling, missing pieces, granule loss.
  • Flashing and seals around chimneys, vents, and valleys - common leak sources.
  • The decking for soft spots or sagging that point to moisture underneath.
  • The attic where accessible - daylight, stains, ventilation, and insulation.
  • Gutters and drainage that affect how water leaves the roof.

How long it takes

Usually under an hour for a typical home, depending on size and access. You don't need to be on the roof - that's our job and our risk, not yours.

What you get

A straight answer - repair, replace, or keep an eye on it - and an honest, itemized quote if work is needed. No pressure, no scare tactics. If a repair will do, we'll say so.

When to book one: if your roof is 15+ years old, after a big storm, before buying or selling a home, or any time you spot a warning sign. Not sure whether you need a fix? See repair vs. replacement.

Free, itemized, and no pressure. We'll tell you straight what your roof needs.
