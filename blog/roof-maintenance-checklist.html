A Simple Roof Maintenance Checklist

Small habits that add years to your roof and head off expensive surprises.

Fluid Roofing · Spokane & Airway Heights, WA

A roof is easy to ignore until it leaks. A little routine attention catches small problems while they're cheap to fix - and helps you get the full lifespan out of the roof you paid for. Here's a simple checklist.

A few times a year

  • Clean the gutters. Clogged gutters back water up under the edge of the roof - a top cause of damage.
  • Clear debris like leaves and branches off the roof, especially in valleys.
  • Trim overhanging branches so they can't scrape shingles or drop limbs in a storm.

From the ground (binoculars help)

  • Look for missing, curling, or cracked shingles.
  • Check for granules collecting in the gutters or at the downspouts.
  • Eyeball the flashing around chimneys and vents.

Inside

  • Check the attic for daylight, water stains, or damp insulation - especially after a big storm.
  • Watch ceilings for new stains.

And once a year: have a professional take a real look, plus after any major storm. A quick inspection now beats a surprise repair later. Don't climb up to do it yourself - it's not worth the risk. Here's what to expect from an inspection.
