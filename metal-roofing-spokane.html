FAQ

Metal Roofing Questions, Answered

Is metal roofing worth it in Spokane?

For many homes, yes - it sheds snow, resists ice, and lasts decades. It costs more up front, so it makes the most sense if you plan to stay in the home a long time.

Is a metal roof noisy in rain?

With proper decking and underlayment underneath, a modern metal roof is no louder than a shingle roof for most homeowners.

How much does metal roofing cost?

Metal runs higher than asphalt shingle - the exact number depends on profile, size, and pitch. We'll give you a free, itemized estimate.
