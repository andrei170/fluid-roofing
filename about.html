Our Story

Local Roots, Real Craftsmanship

Fluid Roofing is the roofing arm of a family-owned company with deep roots in the Spokane-area trades. For years we built our reputation the hard way - one quality job and one happy neighbor at a time - until referrals became our biggest source of work.

That history shapes how we work today: we treat your roof like it's going on a family member's house, because in a town this size, the next customer is usually someone the last one sent our way.
