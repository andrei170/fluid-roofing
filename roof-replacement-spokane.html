FAQ

Roof Replacement Questions, Answered

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are a one-to-three day job once scheduled, weather permitting. We keep the site clean and protect your landscaping.

Do I need a full tear-off or can you go over the old roof?

It depends on the existing layers and decking. A tear-off is usually the better long-term choice; we'll tell you honestly which your roof needs.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Spokane?

Most run roughly $8,000 to $15,000 depending on size, pitch, material, and decking condition. A free on-site estimate gives the exact number.
