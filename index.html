FAQ

Roofing Questions, Answered

How much does a new roof cost in Spokane?

Most residential roof replacements in the Spokane area range from about $8,000 to $15,000 depending on size, pitch, and materials, while repairs are typically far less. The best way to know your number is a free on-site estimate - we'll measure, inspect, and give you an honest, itemized quote. Financing is available.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer flexible monthly payment options so you can protect your home now and spread the cost over time. Ask about plans when you request your free estimate.

Are you licensed and certified?

Fluid Roofing is a GAF-certified, manufacturer-trained roofing contractor serving Washington. Our crews are trained on the systems we install, and our work is backed by signature manufacturer warranties.

Do you handle both commercial and residential roofs?

We do. Fluid Roofing installs and services systems for residential homes and commercial/industrial buildings - from re-roofs to new construction to silicone coating systems.

My roof is leaking - how fast can you help?

Leaks get priority. Call us at (509) 244-1001 or submit the form and we'll get back to you quickly to assess the damage before it spreads.
