Flat Roof Repair in the Spokane Area

The trouble with flat roofs is that water sits instead of running off, so every seam and flashing detail is a potential leak. A fluid-applied coating turns the whole surface into one continuous waterproof membrane - no seams for water to find. It usually costs less than a tear-off and extends the roof's life by years. The one honest caveat: if the insulation underneath is soaked, a coating just traps the problem, so we always check first and tell you straight.

FAQ

Flat Roof Repair Questions, Answered

Can you fix a flat roof that keeps leaking?

Often yes. We diagnose the real source and either repair it or recommend a seamless coating system that seals the whole roof, depending on the roof's condition.

What is a roof coating?

A fluid-applied membrane (commonly silicone) rolled or sprayed over the existing roof to form one seamless, waterproof layer with no seams for water to get through.

Is a coating cheaper than replacing a flat roof?

Usually, yes - you're restoring the roof you have instead of tearing it off. It only works if the structure and insulation underneath are sound, which we check first.
